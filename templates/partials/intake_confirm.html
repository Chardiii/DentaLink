{# Confirmations for the dentist's job order form (New Lab Intake pop-up and the Edit page). Include once after the form. It looks for: form[data-intake-confirm] the job order form (submit buttons name="submit_action", value "send" or "draft") [data-intake-cancel] Cancel / close controls. A link navigates to its href; anything else calls window.closeModal(). Send and Save as draft always ask first. Cancel asks only when something was entered or changed, and offers to save a draft instead. window.IntakeConfirm.requestCancel() lets other code (e.g. clicking outside the pop-up) go through the same check. #}
