1. Purpose of the System

DentaLink is used only to manage dental laboratory work between the Laboratory and its partner clinics: submitting and tracking job orders, sharing case files, monitoring workflow, dispatching finished work, and managing laboratory materials. It may not be used for any other purpose.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your first and last name, display name, mobile number, email address, requested role, and, for dentists, your clinic name.
  • Case information: patient name or reference, case details and tooth selection, shade and instructions, intraoral scan files (such as STL files), and patient photos uploaded with a job order.
  • Activity information: sign-in and sign-out records, including the date and time, IP address, and browser used, kept for security purposes.

3. Responsibilities of Dentists and Clinics

  • You confirm that your patients have consented to sharing their information with the Laboratory for the fabrication of their dental work.

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the information and instructions you submit.

4. Who Can Access Information

  • Dentists can view only the job orders of their own account.
  • Technicians can view the job orders assigned to them and the information needed to complete their tasks.
  • Laboratory administrators can view all records in order to manage cases, accounts, and materials.

The Laboratory does not sell or share your information or your patients' information with third parties, except with the service providers that host the system and send its emails, or when required by law.

5. Account Security
